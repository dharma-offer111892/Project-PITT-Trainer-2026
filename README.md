## 🎮 About the Game

Project P.I.T.T. is a first-person physics sandbox and automation game developed by Froke and published by Pretty Soon. It released on August 19, 2026. Players craft rubber ducks and other products, build chaotic production lines, and feed them to the growing Maw in a satirical brutalist corporate facility.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Project P.I.T.T. uses first-person movement through the corporate facility. Walk, sprint, crouch, jump, and interact with equipment and objects as needed. Trainer options primarily support exploration by removing progression obstacles rather than changing movement controls.

### Tools & Inventory

The game revolves around carrying, placing, and arranging production objects rather than managing a traditional RPG inventory. Use unlimited carry capacity when moving panels or setting up machines, and disable the option before saving if you want the normal handling limits restored.

### World & Environment

Explore the brutalist facility, locate work areas and equipment, and expand production as new tools and products become available. The trainer can accelerate funds and upgrades, allowing you to reach later stages of the facility and observe the Maw's changing production phases sooner.

### Combat & Weapons

Project P.I.T.T. does not feature conventional combat, weapons, or enemy encounters. Gameplay is centered on physics-based construction, automation, product delivery, combo chains, and satisfying the Maw.
